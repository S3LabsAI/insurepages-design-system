# Logos

The InsurePages lockup ("InsurePages by S3 Labs"). Never redraw or recolour it; use these files.

- `insurepages-logo.png` (655×259): the header lockup, for cream or light grounds.
- `insurepages-logo-dark.png` (655×259): the on-`ink` variant for the footer. The master's `#1a1a1a` outline and shadow are remapped to `yellow`, and its `#2c5972` "by … labs" (2.3:1 on ink) to `link-on-ink` #8fc7ff.
- `insurepages-logo-master.png` (800×350, transparent): the master art (`ip-logo.png` at the repo root). The two above are generated from it by `design/build-logo-assets.py`; regenerate, don't hand-edit.
- `favicon.svg`: a `red` rounded tile with an ink stroke and "iP" in `cream`.
